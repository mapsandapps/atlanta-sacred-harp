July 4th and 5th, 1st Sunday and Saturday before, 9:30 am Central Time both days. Henagar-Union Convention, Liberty Church, 2 miles north of Henagar AL on Liberty Road. July 11th, Saturday, 31st annual John C Campbell Folk School Singing, 1 Folk School Rd, Brasstown, NC 28902, Sacred Harp (2025) and Christian Harmony (2010). Location: Davidson Hall Music Studio (2nd floor entrance around back) Campus Map: folkschool.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/campus-map.pdf Introductory session at 9:00am with Esther Morgan-Ellis … Singing: 10:00 AM. - 3:00 PM, Dinner on the grounds at Noon. Come sing, learn the shapes, contemplate the poetry, and relish the power of voices raised together in song. These events welcome first-timer and experienced shape note singers to sing from the tune books Christian Harmony and The Sacred Harp. Experience the beauty of singing 4 parts unaccompanied. All shape note singings are free and open to the public. https://www.folkschool.org/calendar/?trumbaEmbed=view%3Devent%26eventid%3D181481470 Contact: Bill Hogan - hoganbill56 at gmail dot com

We'll sing from four books: The Sacred Harp (1991 Edition), The Sacred Harp (Cooper Revision), The Christian Harmony, and The Colored Sacred Harp. Lunch will be Dutch treat; please plan to bring a sack lunch or enjoy one of Montgomery's tasty restaurants. Please see the full address below: Old Alabama Town, Loeb Reception Center, 301 Columbus Street, Montgomery, AL 36104. Contact - Stefani Priskos - stefani at arts dot alabama dot gov

July 18th (NOTE DATE CHANGE … Saturday before the 3rd Sunday), Saturday, 10:00 am, The 18th Swannanoa Gathering Shape Note Singing, in the Morris Pavilion at Warren Wilson College, Swannanoa, NC, near Asheville. https://www.christianharmony.org/schedule.html Note that Morris Pavilion is an 'open air' pavilion, but is shaded and used for classes taught during the summer.
