October 2026 Atlanta Singing Reminder

Oct 1, First Thursday, singing at 7:00 pm at Emory Presbyterian Church - 1886 N Decatur Rd, Atlanta, GA 30307. Park behind the church, and enter the Fellowship Hall. We'll sing from The Sacred Harp (2025 Revision - loaners are available!) and the Eclectic Harmony. Info: Jim Neal, jimwilliamsneal at gmail dot com

Oct 3, Saturday before the 1st Sunday, 10:00 am, South Georgia Convention, Roberta Civic Center, Roberta GA. Directions: Dinner on the grounds at noon From Atlanta, I-75 South to I-475 South. Turn right/west onto Hwy 80W (Eisenhower Parkway) to Roberta. Or, from Macon, take Hwy 80W to Roberta. In Roberta, turn left/south on N. Mathews Street. It becomes S. Mathews Street when you cross over Agency Street. The Civic Center (an old wooden church with good acoustics), is located at 93 South Mathews Street, just south of the old part of the town, next to the recycling center. S Mathews St is one block east of Highway 341. Contact/info: Oscar McGuire . 478-955-6135 (text preferred) - Contact Oscar with any questions. 2025 Edition.

Oct 3, Saturday, John C. Campbell Folk School - Fall Festival. Shapenote singing workshops in the Jam Pavilion: 11:00 Intro to Shape-Note Singing, 11:30 Christian Harmony Singing, 2:00 Intro to Shape-Note Singing, 2:30 Sacred Harp Singing Contact Esther Morgan-Ellis if interested in attending for entrance and parking information. emellis at gmail dot com

Oct 6, Tuesday, 6:00 pm - 8:00 pm - Sacred Harp Workshop, Covington Branch, DeKalb County Public Library, 3500 Covington Hwy, Decatur, GA 30032. Attendees will learn the basics of melody and harmony singing, get practice reading music using traditional shape note symbols, and discover a rich body of American hymn tunes. Free and open to anyone. No prior musical experience or skill set is needed. Info: Andy Ditzler, andy -at- andyditzler -dot- com

Oct 10, Saturday before the 2nd Sunday - Valdosta All day Singing, Burnt Church Cemetery, 217 E Burnt Church Road, Lakeland, GA 31625. GPS Coordinates: 31° 1.479' N, 83° 2.921' W; singing from the 2025 Sacred Harp (loaners available and a limited number of books for sale). Singing school at 9:30am, singing starts at 10:00am. Dinner on the grounds at noon. Contact - ValdostaSacredHarp at gmail dot com.

Oct 11, 2nd Sunday - The Albright, Cates, Brown, and Sheppard Memorial singing (formerly held at Muscadine Methodist Church in Muscadine, AL) will be held at Holly Springs Church, in Bremen, Georgia. Singing will start at 10:00 EDT. Dinner on the ground will be at 12:00. If you are able, please bring a covered dish. 2025 Edition.

Oct 17, Saturday before the 3rd Sunday, 10:00am - Andrews Schoolhouse, Upson Co. Georgia. 1800 Andrews Chapel Rd, Thomaston, GA 30286-4418. Dinner on the grounds at noon. Contact/info: Oscar McGuire . 478-955-6135 (text preferred) - Contact Oscar with any questions. 2025 Edition.

Oct 18, Sunday, St. Anne's Episcopal Church, 3998 Saint Anne's Lane, NW, Atlanta, GA 30327 - Sacred Harp program for the Adult Formation (Sunday School) at 9:15-:10:15 and then providing music for their worship service at 10:30. Could definitely use some more folks to help with this!!

Oct 19 - Atlanta Singing, 7:00pm. In The Barn behind St. John the Wonderworker 543 Cherokee Ave SE, Atlanta, GA 30312 (near Grant Park and the Atlanta Zoo). The Barn- off of Orleans Avenue, walk up drive way behind the church. No musical experience needed. Contact info: Anna Hinton annajhinton at icloud dot com.

Oct 24, Saturday before 4th Sunday, 10:00 am, Join us for the 48th Annual Seed and Feed Sacred Harp Singing! 10:00am - 3:00pm, Holy Trinity Parish, Tisdale Hall 515 E Ponce de Leon Ave, Decatur, GA 30030, Four-part a cappella shape-note harmony singing from The Sacred Harp. Dinner on the Grounds at Noon. All are welcome (including kids) and loaner songbooks will be available. No singing experience necessary! Contact amyarmstrongmidwife-at-gmail-dot-com with any questions. 2025 Edition.

Oct 31, Saturday - 10:00am, Denney Memorial at Emmaus Primitive Baptist Church on Oak Grove Church Road, Carrollton, GA. Dinner on the grounds at noon. 2025 Edition.
